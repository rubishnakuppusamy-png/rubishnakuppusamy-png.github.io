Week 0 at PRICE Protosem: Stepping Beyond My Comfort Zone

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~3 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Starting something new often comes with a mix of excitement, uncertainty, and curiosity. Week 0 of the PRICE Protosem at Forge Innovation & Ventures was exactly that—a week of stepping outside my comfort zone, meeting new people, and discovering something new every day.

The week began with ice-breakers and team activities that helped us connect with one another and become more comfortable as a group. These activities may have seemed simple at first, but they taught us important lessons about communication, teamwork, and building connections with people from different backgrounds.

Professional Branding & Assessment

The LinkedIn session provided valuable insights into building a professional presence in the digital world. The personality assessment encouraged reflection on our core strengths, characteristics, and areas for improvement.

Storytelling & Creative Expression

The Zen Pencils storytelling session was highly engaging, showcasing how powerful storytelling connects ideas with an audience and encourages us to express our thoughts in a more meaningful way.

The Marshmallow Challenge

This team-building exercise turned into a practical lesson in creativity, communication, and problem-solving. It proved that successful teamwork requires testing ideas, learning from mistakes, and adapting quickly.

Prototyping Environment Tour

Exploring the HW Junction DFab spaces and comprehensive tool boards designed to support our hands-on prototyping projects throughout the programme.

Highlight of the Week

Inaugural Session Keynote

Mr. Kumar Rajagopalan | CEO, Retailers Association of India (RAI)
"His insights into the retail industry provided us with a broader perspective on the sector and helped us understand the importance of innovation, adaptability, and understanding changing consumer needs."

Looking back, Week 0 was more than just an introduction to the programme. It was an opportunity to learn about myself, understand others, and experience different ways of thinking and working together.

I am grateful for the experiences, conversations, and connections from this first week. More importantly, I am excited to continue this journey and make the most of the 20-week PRICE Protosem experience ahead.

✨ Week 0 was just the beginning—and I am looking forward to everything that comes next.

Week 4: Embedded Systems, Sensors, ESP32 and IoT

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~4 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Week 4 of ProtoSem focused on understanding how hardware and software seamlessly work together. We explored embedded systems, Arduino, sensors, ESP32, and the fundamentals of the Internet of Things (IoT). Through practical activities and mini-projects, we learned how to build, program, and control connected electronic systems.

Step 01

Getting Started with Arduino

We began by learning the architectural core of embedded systems and how microcontrollers interact with electronic components. We explored the Arduino IDE, discovering how to write, compile, and flash firmware onto physical development boards.

Hands-on Activity: Programmed digital I/O pins to cycle LEDs ON, OFF, and blink at timed intervals—solidifying how software instructions directly dictate physical state transitions.

ESP32 GPIO 2 LED Circuit Diagram

Physical LED Blink Test on Hardware

Step 02

Exploring Sensors and Actuators

We examined the dual feedback loop of smart systems: sensors collect environmental signals, while actuators execute physical actions driven by the microcontroller's logic.

Sensors (Inputs)

Capture analog and digital environmental variables (temperature, light, proximity).

Actuators (Outputs)

Trigger actions such as motors, buzzers, relays, and optical displays.

Step 03

Working with Arduino Sensors

We connected sensors to Arduino pinouts, processed signals with libraries, and logged live readings in the serial monitor:

Temperature & Humidity Sensor (DHT)

Learned how to detect environmental changes in real-time, parsing calibrated temperature and relative humidity telemetry.

Infrared (IR) Sensor

Explored infrared emitter/receiver pairs for proximity and obstacle detection, communicating digital high/low flags to the board.

DHT11 & Dual Alert LED Wiring Diagram

Live Hardware Interfacing with DHT11 & LED

Step 04

Exploring ESP32 & Interactive Projects

We leveled up to the dual-core ESP32 microcontroller, exploring its advanced pin matrix, processing power, and onboard Wi-Fi / Bluetooth capabilities. We applied these skills in two hands-on projects:

Ultrasonic Range Finder

Built a distance-measuring setup calculating sound wave travel time between trigger pulses and echo returns.

Joystick-Based Game

Engineered an interactive gaming experience combining dual-axis analog joystick inputs, program state machines, and ESP32 responsiveness.

Step 05

Introduction to IoT & Cloud Control

We unpacked the fundamental layers of the Internet of Things (IoT)—perception, network, and application layers—understanding how edge nodes exchange telemetry and receive remote commands over IP networks.

Wi-Fi Controlled Smart LED Circuit

Hooked an LED circuit up to wireless Wi-Fi and interfaced with it digitally. We turned the lighting ON/OFF and dynamically switched color modes across the network using web commands.

ESP32 IoT Telemetry & Status Dashboard

Web & Extension Interface for LED Control

Key Technical Takeaways

Embedded Systems, Microprocessors & Microcontrollers
Arduino IDE Firmware Toolchain & Flashing
Sensors, Actuators & Real-Time Data Collection
Hands-on DHT Temp/Humidity, IR & Ultrasonic Sensors
ESP32 Architecture & Wireless Connectivity
Interactive Joystick Game Logic Development
Fundamentals, Protocols & System Layers of IoT
Hardware-Software-Network Co-Design
My Reflection
"The practical sessions helped me understand how programming can be used beyond software applications to control hardware, collect real-time data, and build connected systems. It was interesting to see how simple electronic components can be combined to create smart and interactive solutions."

Week 5: Design, Technology & Commercialising Innovation

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~5 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Week 5 of ProtoSem focused on UI/UX design, user research, app development, Chrome Extensions, 3D modelling and innovation commercialisation. Through practical activities and expert sessions, I explored how ideas can be transformed into solutions, from identifying problems to designing and developing prototypes.

Step 01

UI/UX Design

We started with the fundamentals of UI (User Interface) and UX (User Experience), learning why empathetic user-centred design is essential for building intuitive, accessible applications.

Hands-on Activity: Given specific user requirements and tasked with designing an application architecture tailored to them. This hands-on challenge demonstrated how to translate user needs into simple, highly functional visual designs, highlighting the importance of usability, accessibility, and clean visual hierarchy.
Step 02

Problem Analysis & User Research

We worked on a real-world problem statement and conducted comprehensive primary user research to deeply understand pain points and validate real user expectations.

Surveys & Interviews

Conducted structured interviews to capture qualitative feedback directly from target users.

Collecting Responses

Gathered and structured data points to detect repeated themes and user frustrations.

Identifying Needs & Challenges

Isolated genuine user friction points from assumed needs to guide solution development.

Analysing Collected Data

Synthesized findings into actionable design insights before touching any code.

Key Takeaway: Emphasized the fundamental necessity of validating problem statements through direct user interaction prior to committing engineering resources.
Step 03

App Design Using Figma

After completing our research synthesis, we used Figma to design an application based on the identified problem.

Converting Research into Digital Solutions

Gained practical experience creating cohesive component systems, typography hierarchies, and intuitive layouts. This bridged the critical link between user research findings and digital prototype design.

Step 04

Chrome Extension Development

We were introduced to Chrome Extensions and explored their purpose, basic manifest structure, background service workers, content scripts, and practical productivity use-cases.

Hands-on Extension Build

As a practical activity, we developed our own custom Chrome Extension. This provided a great opportunity to explore browser-based application development and understand how lightweight extensions enhance everyday web workflows.

Step 05

Fusion 360 & 3D Design

We explored the fundamentals of 3D computer-aided design (CAD) using Autodesk Fusion 360, understanding how spatial concepts transform into accurate digital models ready for additive manufacturing.

Creative Clay Prototyping Activity

We were given modelling clay and complete creative freedom to shape physical prototypes. This exercise encouraged organic experimentation with ergonomics and dimensions, forging a tangible connection between tactile physical prototyping and digital 3D CAD design.

Step 06

Commercialising Innovation

We attended an insightful masterclass: "Commercialising Innovation – From Intellectual Property to International Market" by Mr. Krishna Barad, Director – Global Trade, Ernst & Young LLP (EY).

From IP to International Markets

The session illuminated the pathways to protect intellectual property, formulate go-to-market strategies, and scale solutions internationally. It provided crucial clarity on how prototypes evolve past development into viable commercial market products.

Key Technical & Design Takeaways

Understanding UI/UX principles and user-centred design
Conducting surveys and interviews for user research
Identifying problems and analysing user needs
Designing interactive applications using Figma
Developing custom Chrome Extensions
Exploring Autodesk Fusion 360 & 3D CAD modelling
Creating rapid physical prototypes using clay
Understanding IP protection & innovation commercialisation
My Reflection
"Week 5 helped me understand how different skills come together in the innovation process. From user research and app design to 3D modelling and commercialisation, each activity gave me a different perspective on developing solutions. The practical sessions improved my understanding of design and prototyping, while the expert session introduced me to the business side of innovation. Overall, it was a valuable week that connected creativity, technology and entrepreneurship, helping me understand how an idea can progress from a problem to a potential market-ready solution."
