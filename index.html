PRICE PROTOSEM ARCHIVE WEEK 00 • PUBLISHED

Week 0 at PRICE Protosem: Stepping Beyond My Comfort Zone

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~3 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Starting something new often comes with a mix of excitement, uncertainty, and curiosity. Week 0 of the PRICE Protosem at Forge Innovation & Ventures was exactly that—a week of stepping outside my comfort zone, meeting new people, and discovering something new every day.

The week began with ice-breakers and team activities that helped us connect with one another and become more comfortable as a group. These activities may have seemed simple at first, but they taught us important lessons about communication, teamwork, and building connections with people from different backgrounds.

Professional Branding & Assessment

The LinkedIn session provided valuable insights into building a professional presence in the digital world. The personality assessment encouraged reflection on our core strengths, characteristics, and areas for improvement.

Storytelling & Creative Expression

The Zen Pencils storytelling session was highly engaging, showcasing how powerful storytelling connects ideas with an audience and encourages us to express our thoughts in a more meaningful way.

The Marshmallow Challenge

This team-building exercise turned into a practical lesson in creativity, communication, and problem-solving. It proved that successful teamwork requires testing ideas, learning from mistakes, and adapting quickly.

Prototyping Environment Tour

Exploring the HW Junction DFab spaces and comprehensive tool boards designed to support our hands-on prototyping projects throughout the programme.

Highlight of the Week

Inaugural Session Keynote

Mr. Kumar Rajagopalan | CEO, Retailers Association of India (RAI)
"His insights into the retail industry provided us with a broader perspective on the sector and helped us understand the importance of innovation, adaptability, and understanding changing consumer needs."

Looking back, Week 0 was more than just an introduction to the programme. It was an opportunity to learn about myself, understand others, and experience different ways of thinking and working together.

I am grateful for the experiences, conversations, and connections from this first week. More importantly, I am excited to continue this journey and make the most of the 20-week PRICE Protosem experience ahead.

✨ Week 0 was just the beginning—and I am looking forward to everything that comes next.

Week 1: Learning Beyond the Classroom

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~4 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Week 1 of PROTOSEM was all about exploring, experimenting, and truly learning beyond the conventional classroom walls.

Design Thinking & Vision

Through Design Thinking I learned a crucial lesson: understand the problem before building the solution. Alongside this, the Vision & Goals session provided a valuable opportunity to reflect on where I currently am and where I want to go in my career.

Exploring the Gaming Industry

As part of the Beta Team, we explored the Gaming Industry through its evolution, ecosystem, Value Chain, and SWOT Analysis. This deep dive helped me look at an industry far beyond its products and truly understand the business perspective and strategy behind it.

Tech Talks & Portfolio Building

We explored Antigravity and GitHub while beginning to build our portfolios—a dedicated space to showcase our skills, projects, and individuality. The industry interactions and Tech Talks on Recommendation Algorithms, Power BI, Prospect Theory, and Base44 gave me a concrete understanding of how technology, data, and human behaviour intricately connect with real-world businesses.

My Biggest Takeaway
"Learning is not about gaining knowledge. It is about questioning, experimenting, creating, adapting, and applying."

One week down. More lessons to go.

🚀 Not just building a portfolio. Building the person behind it.
PRICE PROTOSEM ARCHIVE WEEK 02 • PUBLISHED

Week 2: From Idea to Prototype

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~3 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Week 2 of PROTOSEM introduced us to MIT App Inventor, a visual tool that makes building mobile applications accessible and intuitive. The most exciting part of this week was seeing our idea move from a pure concept to an actual working prototype and getting tangible results from our implementation.

Ollipadai – Smart Print Ordering System

We learned the core basics of app development and immediately used them to build our idea, Ollipadai, into a working prototype. We also spent time actively testing our progress using the MIT AI2 Companion, ensuring our blocks and logic executed seamlessly.

Our final functional prototype included:

  • Student login and authentication
  • Order configuration (color mode, sides, copies)
  • Document upload capabilities
  • An admin/shopkeeper dashboard to accept, reject, and manage incoming orders
My Key Takeaway
"Week 2 taught me that even a simple idea can become a practical solution when we learn the right tools and apply them effectively."

Learning the tool Building the prototype Testing the idea Seeing the results

From theory to a hands-on step towards turning ideas into real solutions.

📱 Excited to explore more tools and build even more impactful products!
PRICE PROTOSEM ARCHIVE WEEK 03 • PUBLISHED

Week 3: Completing the App & Organising the Workspace

Forge Innovation & Ventures ~3 Min Read Rubishna Kuppusamy

Week 3 was heavily focused on turning our prototype into a fully working application and implementing best practices in our physical workspace.

Completing Ollipadai

We completed the Ollipadai – Smart Print Ordering System and successfully developed both the Student/User interface and the Admin interface. We connected these core features and thoroughly tested the app to ensure the workflow operated smoothly—from placing a print order to managing it on the shopkeeper side.

5S Methodology & Organising Workspace

Along with app development, we were introduced to the 5S methodology and applied it directly while organising the electrical components. Sorting and systematically arranging these components helped us truly understand the critical importance of maintaining an organised, efficient, and easily accessible workspace.

My Key Takeaway
"Week 3 taught me that building a solution is not just about creating it — it is also about testing, organising, improving, and making it work in practice."

Week 3 was all about turning our ideas into something more complete and practical.
